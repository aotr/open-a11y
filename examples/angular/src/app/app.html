Accessibility that fits the page, not the other way around.

Angular 21

The Angular adapter uses signals and a small mount helper. Register the engine with provideAccessibility(engine), then call mountAccessibilityWidget(service, options) after the view exists. Changes remount without losing accessibility state.

Signals all the way down

AccessibilityService exposes settings and states as signals. The directive uses the same process-wide mount registry as Vanilla, React, Vue, and Svelte.

  • Angular 17+ standalone directive.
  • Signal-based service.
  • No TestBed required for adapter tests.
